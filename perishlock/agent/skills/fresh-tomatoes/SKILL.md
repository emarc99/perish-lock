# Fresh Roma Tomatoes — Post-Harvest Cold-Chain & Salvage Protocol

Follow these guidelines when evaluating cold-room incidents and optimizing salvage allocations for fresh Roma tomatoes (*Solanum lycopersicum*).

## 1. Storage Biology & Critical Temperature Boundaries
- **Optimal Range:** 10.0°C to 12.5°C (50°F - 55°F) at 90–95% Relative Humidity.
- **Chilling Injury Threshold:** Strictly below 10.0°C. Prolonged exposure causes surface pitting, mealiness, incomplete ripening, and rapid decay upon warming.
- **Accelerated Senescence & Microbial Rot Threshold:** Above 13.0°C (55.4°F). Ethylene production spikes, softening accelerates, and Alternaria/Botrytis fungal germination doubles for every 3°C rise.
- **Critical Thermal Failure:** Above 18.0°C. Flash-softening occurs; crop is ineligible for fresh wholesale within 12 hours.

## 2. USDA Grade Standards & Destination Capabilities
- **USDA U.S. No. 1:** Firm, mature, well developed, clean, fairly well formed, free from decay, freezing, sunscald, and severe bruising.
  - *Eligible Destinations:* Premium Wholesale, Retail Supermarkets, Metro Terminals.
- **USDA Combination / U.S. No. 2:** Mature tomatoes with minor skin checks, light cat-facing, or slight softness, but sound interior.
  - *Eligible Destinations:* Community Food Banks, Local Farmers Markets, Canneries.
- **Processing Grade:** Overripe, soft, skin cracking, or color irregularities, free of active watery rot or mold.
  - *Eligible Destinations:* Industrial Tomato Paste, Puree, Sauces, Sun-Dried Lines.
- **Cull / Biogas:** Liquid leakage, black mold, microbial rot > 2%.
  - *Eligible Destinations:* Composting, biodigestion, non-food animal feed.

Always cross-reference unstructured intake notes against live incident telemetry:
1. **Operating Shifts & Dock Closures:** If a cannery note states "dock B closes at 18:00 due to boiler maintenance", compute transit time. If arrival is after 18:00, do NOT allocate to that dock today.
2. **Quality Exclusions:** If a wholesale terminal requires firm-ripe fruit and core temp < 15.5°C, check chamber peak temperature. If chamber exceeded 16.5°C for > 2 hours, wholesale will reject the load at the dock (incurring $150 unloading penalty). Direct to processing or food rescue instead.
3. **Food Rescue Capacity:** Community food rescue banks often have volunteer sorting crews with tight windows (e.g. 13:00–17:00) and limited refrigeration (max 5 metric tons). Allocate within their specific volume caps.
4. **Logistics & Ambient Heat:** If ambient temperature exceeds 32°C (heatwave alert), unrefrigerated transit is lethal. All dispatches must mandate refrigerated reefer units.
